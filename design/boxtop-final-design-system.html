Motion

"Vibrant and prominent" is a layout and imagery job more than a motion job. Spend boldness on scale, colour and type; keep movement quiet and purposeful.

Do

  • One satisfying add-to-hamper confirmation (icon-count bump, brief toast) — a single moment, not several competing ones
  • Hover lift + shadow on product cards, under (hover: hover) only
  • Respect prefers-reduced-motion: the duration tokens above zero out automatically
  • Reserve space with aspect-ratio so nothing shifts as images load

Don't

  • Fade-and-slide-up on every section as it scrolls into view — the generic default, and it fights "no horizontal scroll, no layout jump" on slow connections
  • Autoplay carousels or hero slideshows
  • Hover-only content with no touch equivalent
  • Motion as a substitute for a confident layout — a big headline and real product photography carry more "prominent" than any animation will
